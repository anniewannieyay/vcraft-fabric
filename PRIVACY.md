## What VCraft stores on the relay

| Data | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Minecraft username and UUID | To identify you and let friends find you | Until you delete your account |
| Your public encryption key | So friends can encrypt messages to you | Until you delete your account |
| Friends, friend requests, blocks, groups | So these work across devices and sessions | Until you delete your account |
| Your status and when you were last online | Presence and unread counts | Until you delete your account |
| Direct and group messages, **end-to-end encrypted** | Offline delivery and history | Up to 300 per conversation, deleted after [365] days |
| VCraft Community (public chat) messages, **not encrypted** | The public chat | Latest 500 messages, deleted after [365] days |

Message contents and voice calls in direct messages, groups and calls are end-to-end encrypted:
the operator **cannot** read or listen to them. The relay can see who talks to whom and when.

Voice is relayed live and never stored. IP addresses are used to limit abuse while you're connected
and are not stored.

Sign-in is verified with Mojang/Microsoft's session servers, the same way any Minecraft server does.
Your Microsoft access token never leaves your computer.

## Your rights

- **Download your data:** VCraft → Settings → Privacy → *Download my data*.
- **Delete your account:** VCraft → Settings → Privacy → *Delete account*. This permanently removes
  your account, relationships and stored messages from the relay.
- If you can't sign in, email [email] and we'll delete your data manually.

## Moderation

The operator can ban accounts, and moderators can mute people and remove messages in the public
VCraft Community chat. Private messages can't be moderated because they're encrypted; use *Block*.

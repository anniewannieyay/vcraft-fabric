# VCraft test checklist

Use 2–3 players on **different networks**, connected to the same relay. Run through it once on
**1.21.11** and once on **26.3**, and try one round where one player is on each version.
Note anything odd, with a screenshot and `logs/latest.log`.

## Setup
- [ ] Mod loads; the **VCraft** button appears on the title and pause screens, and **O** opens it
- [ ] It connects and shows your name; your face shows as your skin
- [ ] The relay address can be changed in Settings and it reconnects

## Friends & status
- [ ] Send, accept, decline and cancel friend requests
- [ ] Remove a friend; block and unblock someone
- [ ] Online / Idle / Do Not Disturb / Offline show correctly to the other player
- [ ] Do Not Disturb silences pop-ups, the ding and the ringtone

## Messages
- [ ] DM back and forth; the **ding** plays and a pop-up shows
- [ ] Reply to a message, delete your own message (it disappears for both), scroll back through history
- [ ] Message someone who's offline; they see it with an unread badge when they sign in
- [ ] Long messages wrap and don't run off the screen

## Groups
- [ ] Create a group, add someone, rename it, remove someone (owner only), leave it
- [ ] Group messages reach everyone

## Voice
- [ ] Settings → Test microphone shows the meter and plays back your voice
- [ ] Call a friend: the **disc ringtone** plays; answer and hear each other clearly
- [ ] Decline a call, and let one ring out for 60 seconds
- [ ] Mute (M), deafen (N) and push-to-talk (V) work; "Mute for me" works
- [ ] Invite a third person into a call already in progress; call a whole group
- [ ] Hang up; the overlay in-game shows who's talking

## Community & ranks
- [ ] The VCraft Community tab opens; messages appear for everyone; slow mode kicks in
- [ ] dufeaver shows the **Owner** badge; staff can delete messages and mute people
- [ ] A blocked person's community messages are hidden

## Server invites
- [ ] On a multiplayer server, send an invite; the other player gets a confirm prompt and joins

## Encryption & privacy
- [ ] Settings → Verify encryption shows the same safety number for both players
- [ ] Download my data saves a file; Delete account removes you from friends' lists

## Stability
- [ ] Restart the relay mid-session; clients reconnect on their own
- [ ] No crashes after 30+ minutes, including switching worlds and servers during a call
